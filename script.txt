The day Paul Reston shook my hand and called me the most talented analyst he'd ever worked with, I believed him. That was my first mistake. My second was letting him be the only person in that building who knew exactly how good I was.

I came to Hargrove & Associates three years out of Northwestern with a finance degree, a minor in statistics, and the kind of focus that made my college roommates call me "the monk." Not because I was quiet — I talked plenty — but because when I locked onto something, nothing else existed.
