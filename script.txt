If your salary keeps increasing but your bank account doesn't... you're making this one expensive mistake.

Most people spend first and save whatever is left. 

Wealthy people do the opposite. The moment they get paid, they automatically move 20% into investments or savings.

Then they live on the remaining 80%. It's called "Pay Yourself First," and it's one of the simplest habits behind long-term wealth.

You don't need a higher salary to start building wealth. 

You need a better system. Start with just 10% today, and let consistency do the work.
